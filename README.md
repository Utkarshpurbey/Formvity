# Formvity

**Build forms. Understand every response.**

[formvity.in](https://formvity.in) is a form platform for teams who need more than a survey link — design beautiful multi-page forms, publish a shareable link, and see who responded and how, all in one place.

Respondents never need an account. They open your link, complete a short intake step if you want one, fill out the form, and submit.

---

## Why Formvity?

Most form tools stop at collecting answers. Formvity helps you go from idea to insight:

- **Build faster** — Start from 20+ ready-made templates or design from scratch with a visual drag-and-drop editor. No code required.
- **Look professional** — Customize colors, typography, and layout so every form matches your brand.
- **Share instantly** — Publish with one click and send a clean public link. Update live forms when your questions change.
- **Understand responses** — See timelines, audience breakdowns, per-question charts, and individual submissions — not just a spreadsheet export.
- **Work as a team** — Organize forms in workspaces, invite colleagues as editors or viewers, and keep drafts separate from what’s live.

---

## Who is it for?

**Form makers** — HR teams running onboarding surveys, sales capturing leads, event organizers collecting registrations, support desks triaging requests, educators managing enrollments, or anyone who needs structured data from people outside their company.

**Respondents** — Anyone with a link. No signup, no app install, no friction.

---

## How it works

1. **Create** — Pick a template or start blank. Add pages, fields, validation, and an optional intake step (name, email, custom fields).
2. **Publish** — Save your draft, then publish when you’re ready. Copy the link and share it anywhere.
3. **Learn** — Watch responses come in. Open analytics to spot trends, compare audiences, and read each submission in full.
